Rummy score sheet

A score sheet to print for rummy at a table: eleven rounds and up to seven players, with the cards dealt and the wild rank in each round, as Airplane Rummy plays them.

The sheet

Write each player's name at the top of a column. In each box, write that player's total so far: the last total plus this round's points.

Points for each loose card

  • 3 to 10: the number on the card. Jack 11, Queen 12, King 13.
  • A card of the round's wild rank: 20. A Joker: 50.
  • Cards in sets and runs: 0.
  • Whoever goes out scores 0 for the round. The lowest total after round 11 wins.

For another rummy game

The Cards and Wild columns follow Airplane Rummy's eleven rounds: round 1 deals 3 cards and threes are wild, and each round deals one more card, up to 13 with Kings wild. For another rummy game, leave those two columns blank, use the rows for up to eleven hands and count your game's own points.

In the game

Airplane Rummy keeps this sheet for you. After each round it shows every meld, every loose card and how each total adds up, such as 115 + 27 = 142. It is free on the App Store for iPhone and iPad, with no ads and no account, and it works offline.
