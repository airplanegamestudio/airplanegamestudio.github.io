Airplane Rummy privacy policy

Effective 7 November 2026

Airplane Rummy runs entirely on your phone or tablet. It collects no data.

What the app does not do

  • It never asks for an account, a name or an email address.
  • It never connects to the internet for anything of its own. It has no server and no network permission, makes no network requests and downloads nothing. The one thing it asks your phone for is an age range, explained below.
  • It shows no ads and has no analytics, tracking or crash reporting.
  • It sells nothing, so there are no purchases or subscriptions.
  • It never asks for your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or notifications.

What stays on your device

Three things are stored, all on your device only.

Your match. The cards, scores, round and turn of the match in progress, so the game can resume where you stopped. It is kept in the app's private storage, left out of iCloud and Google backups, and removed when you delete the app or start a new match. It holds no name of yours; the computer players at the table have fixed, made-up names.

Your sound setting. Whether you turned card sounds off. It is one yes-or-no value kept in the app's preferences. On iPhone and iPad it can be included in your device backup. On Android it is left out of Google backup but may travel in a device-to-device transfer on some phones. Deleting the app removes it.

Your table size. How many players you last chose for a new match, from two to seven. It is one number kept beside your match in the app's private storage, left out of iCloud and Google backups, and removed when you delete the app.

None of it is sent anywhere.

Your age range

Some U.S. states require apps to check a player's age range. On iPhone and iPad, the game first asks Apple whether such a law applies to you, and asks for your age range only if it does. On Android, the game asks Google Play, which shares a range only where a law applies or where you have chosen in Play to share it. Your phone may go online to answer; the game itself sends nothing. The answer is a broad range, never a birthdate. The game keeps it only in memory while the check runs, never saves it, never sends it, and plays the same whatever it says, or if there is no answer at all. The game works fully when started offline, for example in airplane mode.

If you email support

Email is outside the app. If you write to airplanegamestudio@gmail.com, we receive your address and whatever you include, and use it only to reply. Please do not send passwords, payment details, ID documents or private photos. Messages are deleted within twelve months of the last reply unless an open issue or the law needs them longer. They are never sold or used for advertising, and nobody processes them except the email service that delivers them.

Children

Airplane Rummy is made for adults. It is not in Apple's Kids category or Google Play's Families program, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes

If a future version ever collects data, this policy will change before that version is released, and the policy inside the app will say the same as this page.
