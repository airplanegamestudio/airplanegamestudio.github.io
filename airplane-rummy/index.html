A rummy card game with five suits, for iPhone and iPad. You play against computer players, offline, so it works in airplane mode with no Wi-Fi needed.

How to play

A match is eleven rounds. The first round deals three cards and each round deals one more, up to thirteen in the last. The card that matches the hand size is wild: threes in the first round, kings in the last. Jokers are always wild.

On your turn, take the face-up card from the discard pile or draw from the deck. Arrange your cards into sets (three or more of one rank) and runs (three or more in a row in one suit), then discard one card.

When every card you keep is in a set or a run, you can go out. Everyone else then gets one final turn. Cards left over count against you, and the lowest total after eleven rounds wins.

The deck is two packs of 58 cards, 116 in all: 3 through King in each of the five suits, plus six Jokers.

Learn as you play

A short guide walks you through your first hand in four pages, and you can skip it. After each round, the score sheet shows every set and run, every leftover card and how the points add up, so you can see where your score came from. While you arrange your hand, you can undo a move before you discard.

Who it is for

Adults who like rummy and want a card game that works without a signal: on a flight, on a train, or anywhere the Wi-Fi is bad. A match takes about fifteen to twenty minutes. The game saves as you play, so you can close it and pick up where you stopped.

Privacy

The game collects no data. Your match and your settings stay on your device. The privacy policy has the details, including the age-range check that some U.S. states require.
